1. **Service**
2. **Fraternity**
3. **Marian Inspiration**
4. **Mercy**

## Our Lady of Sorrows

The Servite Order's central devotion is to the Sorrowful Virgin at the
foot of the Cross. This devotion traces back to the Order's founding
story: tradition holds that the Seven Holy Founders saw visions of Mary
pierced by seven swords of sorrow.

Two dates mark this devotion in the Order's calendar:

- The **Fifth Friday of Lent**, commemorating Mary at the Cross
- **15 September**, the Feast of Our Lady of Sorrows — the principal feast
  of the whole Servite Family

## The Black Habit

Servite friars and nuns wear a black habit as a sign of mourning — for
Christ's Passion and for Mary's sorrows. St. Philip Benizi, an early
friar and theologian who led the Order as Prior General from 1267, is
remembered for explaining the habit's significance.
