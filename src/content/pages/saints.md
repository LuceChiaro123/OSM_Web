## St. Philip Benizi

An early Servite friar and theologian. Elected Prior General of the Order
in 1267, he is remembered in particular for explaining the significance of
the Order's black habit.

## Other Notable Figures

These figures are historically significant to the Order; the brief does
not indicate that either has been canonized or beatified.

- **Paolo Sarpi** — theological advisor to the Republic of Venice and
  historian of the Council of Trent.
- **Arcangelo Giani** — published the *Annales Ordinis Servorum* (in
  volumes issued 1618–1622), an early history of the Order.
