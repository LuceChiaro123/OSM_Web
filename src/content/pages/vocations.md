## Discerning a Vocation

The Servite Order lives by four pillars — Service, Fraternity, Marian
Inspiration, and Mercy — under the motto: *"Servants of the Lord and their
brothers and sisters, following the model of Mary, the Mother of God."*
Those discerning a vocation are invited to explore religious life within
this Marian, community-centered charism.
