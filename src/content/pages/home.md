## Welcome

The Servite Order (Ordo Servorum Mariae, O.S.M.) — the Servants of Mary —
is one of the original five mendicant orders of the Catholic Church,
founded in 1233. This site serves the Order's community in Indonesia, with
houses in Ruteng, Malang, Kalimantan Barat, and Tangerang Selatan — see
[Our Communities](/communities/) for details.
