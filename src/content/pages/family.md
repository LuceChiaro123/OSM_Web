The Servite Family lives by the Rule of St. Augustine together with its
own Constitutions (current version approved 1987), and is made up of
several branches:

## Friars

Priests and brothers — the original order, founded by the Seven Holy
Founders in 1233.

## Contemplative Nuns

Servite nuns living a contemplative, enclosed religious life.

## Sisters

A congregation of active religious sisters engaged in apostolic works.

## Secular Order & Institutes

Lay members participate through the **Servite Secular Order**. Related
secular institutes include the Servite Secular Institute in England and
Regnum Mariae in Italy.
